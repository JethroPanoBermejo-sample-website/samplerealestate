Before you visit

What to check between finding a listing and walking through the door.

What should I compare across properties?

Start with the asking price, usable floor area and location. For a condo, check dues and whether the price includes parking. For a house or townhome, ask about lot size and who maintains shared areas.

How do I request a viewing?

Open a property’s details and choose Request a viewing. Select a preferred time and try the form with sample contact details.

What should I look for at the viewing?

Check natural light, ventilation, power outlets, storage and street noise. Walk through the parking spot and bring a tape measure for any furniture you plan to keep.
